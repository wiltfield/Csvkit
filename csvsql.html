csvsql

Generate a CREATE TABLE and INSERT statements from your CSV, or insert it directly into a connected database.

In Generate SQL mode you can find up to 18 supported dialects, including ones supported in the original csvkit, with the addition of a few others that may be niche or old, though you'd have to copy the output and push it manually in your own database's SQL editor. Alternatively, in Connect & push to DB mode you can connect your database and push directly into it, four dialects are supported in this mode: Postgres, MySQL, CockroachDB, and MariaDB - that should cover most users out there.
